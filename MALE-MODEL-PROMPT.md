# Male Model — Image Prompt

Everything you need for his images. Female model is in a separate file.

---

## HOW TO USE THIS (ChatGPT)

1. Open ChatGPT and start **one chat** for this model. Keep using the same chat —
   do not start a new one each time.
2. **Attach his reference photo** to the message. Every message, not just the first.
3. Copy the prompt below and paste it under the photo.
4. Before pasting, change the **three → lines** (outfit, pose, detail).
5. Send. Download the image. Crop to the size you need.

**If you skip step 2 you will get a different man who merely looks similar.**
Text cannot hold a face. The attached photo is what holds it.

**When you get a really good image, save it and use that as the new reference
photo from then on.** Quality drifts if you keep going back to the original.

His glasses and earring are the two things that make him recognisable. The AVOID
line below protects them — the model will drop them otherwise.

## THE PROMPT — copy from here

```
Keep the exact same face, hair, glasses and body from the attached photo.
Change only the clothing.

SUBJECT: Man in his mid twenties, mixed race, medium-brown skin with a warm
undertone and natural texture. Dark brown tightly curled hair, short at the sides,
a few inches of loose curls on top. Thin round pale-gold wire-frame glasses. Fine
moustache and a small neat chin beard. Small stud earring in one ear. Oval face,
dark brown eyes, calm neutral expression. Slim build, narrow frame.

OUTFIT: Tan pleated cotton shorts with turn-up cuffs, a cream cricket knit with
navy striped trim, white crew socks and black leather penny loafers.

POSE: Standing straight to camera, both hands loosely in pockets.

DETAIL: One sleeve pushed slightly higher than the other.

SETTING: Cream panelled wall with simple wainscoting, herringbone parquet floor in
warm oak.

LIGHT: Soft even daylight from camera left, gentle shadow on the wall behind, no
harsh contrast.

CAMERA: Full body, head to shoes, nothing cropped. Shot on 85mm at f/4, eye level.

STYLE: Editorial menswear photography. Natural colour, realistic fabric drape,
visible texture in knitwear and cotton. No beauty retouching.

FORMAT: Vertical 2:3.

AVOID: Changing the face. Removing the glasses. Removing the earring. Altering
hair texture. Cartoon or 3D render look. Plastic skin. Extra fingers. Logos or
brand names. Text or watermarks. Harsh flash. The same pose as the previous image.
Perfect symmetry.
```

### 3. DETAIL

One per image. This is what stops it looking generated.

- a soft crease at the elbow and behind the knee where the fabric has been sitting
- one sleeve pushed slightly higher than the other
- the shirt hem sitting slightly uneven
- a shoe turned very slightly inward, natural stance
- the collar sitting a little open on one side
- fabric caught mid-movement, a little lift at the hem

## CHANGING THE SIZE FOR DIFFERENT PARTS OF THE SITE

The prompt above gives 2:3, which covers most slots. When you need something else,
swap the CAMERA and FORMAT lines.

| Where it goes | Save as | FORMAT line | CAMERA line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage hero | 1000 × 1500 | `Vertical 2:3.` | `Full body, head to shoes, nothing cropped.` |
| Guide card | 900 × 1200 | `Vertical 3:4.` | `Full body, head in the upper third of the frame.` |
| Men's panel | 900 × 1200 | `Vertical 3:4.` | `Framed from the knee up, headroom above.` |
| Pinterest pin | 1000 × 1500 | `Vertical 2:3.` | `Full body with clear space at the top for a text overlay.` |
| Inside an article | 1000 × 1500 | `Vertical 2:3.` | `Full body, head to shoes, shoes clearly visible.` |

**The men's panel is the one to watch.** On the site a caption sits over the bottom
quarter of that picture, so a full-length shot loses its shoes and half the
trousers. Frame that one from the knee up.

**Inside an article, always show the shoes.** Half of styling advice is the shoes.

## THE ONE RULE THAT MATTERS MOST

Keep the **wall and the light identical** through a whole guide. Fifteen outfits in
fifteen rooms looks like a Pinterest board. Fifteen against the same wall looks
like a shoot — and that is what makes a new site read as a real publication.

Change the room between guides if you like. Never inside one.
