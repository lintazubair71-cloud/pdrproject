**Article:** How to Style Princess Dress for Women: 15 Outfits
**URL slug:** princess-dress-for-women
**Audience:** Women

## Notes

- Every person-image is ONE vertical 2:3 image in two parts: LEFT a mirror selfie (phone fully hides the face), RIGHT a flat-lay of the same outfit. Plus ONE horizontal 1200×700 WebP featured flat-lay.
- The **princess dress is the hero** in every look — the dress leads and accessories are kept to exactly what the article names (restraint is the article's one rule).
- Shoes named in every look (house rule); where a look does not name a shoe, the article's own rule applies → nude heels (metallic on #3, #6; sparkle on #13; ballet flats on #5 as named).
- One consistent model line: slim young woman, mid-twenties, medium-brown skin, dark hair — hair **styled per the article** (swept up #3, loose + pearl headband #5, sleek updo #9, sleek bun #14, soft side-swept waves otherwise). #11 petite, #12 curvy, as the article specifies.
- Poses rotate (01–06), never two the same in a row. Background colour rotates, never two the same in a row, chosen so the gown does not blend into the wall.

**Colour/fabric picks** (article left most gown colours open): #1 midnight-navy tulle · #2 dusty-rose A-line (no white/ivory/champagne — wedding rule) · #3 lilac tulle · #4 deep-burgundy satin · #5 pale-blue pastel · #6 pewter/liquid-silver satin · #7 black two-piece · #8 emerald + gold jewellery (article example) · #9 ruby gown + ivory satin gloves · #10 plum gown + ivory faux-fur wrap · #11 dusty-blue · #12 sapphire sweetheart · #13 bright fuchsia · #14 clean charcoal satin · #15 dramatic red.
