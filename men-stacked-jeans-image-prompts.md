# How to Style Men Stacked Jeans — Image-Prompt Pack

> #6 flannel → red/black checked · #7 blazer → navy · #10 hoodie → grey, bomber → black · #13 knitted polo → navy · #15 hoodie → grey, wool overcoat → camel.
> **Accessories:** the article names none per look, so every image has none.
> **Graphic tees (#3, #12):** kept as abstract, no-text prints (watermark rule).
> The key look — a slim-to-straight leg cut long so the denim stacks and folds over the shoe at the ankle — is written into every prompt, with shoes kept clearly visible.
