## Notes

- Every person-image is ONE vertical 2:3 image in two parts: LEFT a mirror selfie (phone fully hides the face), RIGHT a flat-lay of the same outfit. Plus ONE horizontal 1200×700 WebP featured flat-lay.
- The **chunky shoe is the hero** in every look, so each prompt keeps the shoe clearly visible with its **thick chunky / lug sole**, and the hem breaking cleanly at the top of the shoe (the article's whole point).
- One consistent model line across the article: slim young man, mid-twenties, medium-brown skin, dark tousled hair.
- Poses rotate (01–06), never two the same in a row. Background colour rotates, never two the same in a row.
- **Socks** shown only where the article names them (look 8 — white crew sock). **No other accessories** — the article names none.

**Colour/type picks** (where the article left a piece open): #2 white sneakers, stone trousers, grey knit · #3 white sneakers, grey joggers+hoodie, black bomber · #4 brown lug boots, mid-blue jeans, checked flannel · #5 white sneakers, stone chinos, light-blue Oxford, navy bomber · #6 brown lug loafers, taupe trousers, cream knit · #7 white sneakers (black jacket) · #8 white sneakers, stone shorts, white tee + white crew sock · #9 white sneakers, mid-blue jeans, white tee · #10 brown brogues, charcoal wool trousers, oatmeal knit, olive overshirt · #11 white sneakers, khaki cargos, stone tee · #12 tee white · #13 white sneakers · #14 black Chelsea lug boots, black everything · #15 all-stone (off-white chunky sneakers).
