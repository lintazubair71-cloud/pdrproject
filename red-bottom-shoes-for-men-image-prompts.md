# Red Bottom Shoes for Men — Image-Prompt Pack

**Article:** How to Style Red Bottom Shoes for Men
**URL slug:** red-bottom-shoes-for-men
**Audience:** Men

## Notes

- Every person-image is ONE vertical 2:3 image in two parts: LEFT a mirror selfie (phone fully hides the face), RIGHT a flat-lay of the same outfit. Plus ONE horizontal 1200×700 WebP featured flat-lay.
- The **red-soled shoe is the hero** in every look, so each prompt keeps the shoe clearly visible with the **glossy red lacquered sole showing**. Everything else stays dark/neutral — never a second hit of red anywhere but the soles (the article's rule).
- **No brand name or logo is drawn** (per the no-logos rule) — just the red-soled shoe itself.
- **No accessories** — the article names none.
- One consistent model line across the article: slim young man, mid-twenties, medium-brown skin, dark tousled hair.
- Poses rotate (01–06), never two the same in a row. Background colour rotates, never two the same in a row.

**Colour/type picks** (where the article left a shoe's colour or type open): #2 jacket black · #4 oxfords black · #5 velvet black · #7 studded sneakers black · #8 Chelsea boots black · #9 chinos stone · #10 loafers black, shorts stone, polo navy · #12 burgundy loafers · #14 tuxedo black. "Shoes" (generic) → loafers.

**Backgrounds:** no red/terracotta backgrounds, so nothing competes with the red sole.
