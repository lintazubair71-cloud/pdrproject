# How to Style Leather Pants Men — Image-Prompt Pack

> Every look keeps the leather as the one statement: a plain soft top, a slim-straight fit (written into each prompt), and everyday shoes (Chelsea boots, loafers, derbies or clean sneakers) — never combat/biker boots, per the article.
> **Colour picks** (article names none — neutral picks): #8 → chambray shirt · #9 blazer navy, loafers black · #10 flannel → olive check (from "rust/olive/check") · #13 bomber khaki, sneakers white · #14 overcoat charcoal, knit grey. Leather colour: named — #4, #11 brown · all others black.
> **No accessories** — the article names none. #15 graphic tee kept as an abstract, no-text print (watermark rule).
