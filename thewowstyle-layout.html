How to Style Khaki Shorts Women: 15 Outfits

Khaki shorts are the most useful thing in a summer wardrobe and the easiest to get wrong. The difference is almost always length and what you put on your feet.

In this guide

  1. Getting the length right
  2. 15 outfits, styled
  3. What to wear on your feet
  4. Questions people ask

Before the outfits: khaki is a neutral that leans warm, so it sits better with cream, rust, olive and denim than with cool greys. Everything below is built from that one rule.

Rule 1 — link UP to the pillar in the first 150 words. One link only, anchored on the pillar's own phrase: "…see our full how to style library". Not three links, not "click here".

Questions people ask

What length is most flattering?

Just above the knee suits most heights. If you are under 5'4", a 4–5 inch inseam keeps the leg line unbroken.

Can you wear khaki shorts to work?

In a business-casual office, yes — with a collared shirt and closed shoes. See our dress codes guide for where the line sits.

What colours go with khaki?

Cream, white, rust, olive, navy and denim. Avoid cool greys, which make khaki look muddy.

FAQ earns its place. 78% of your keywords show a People Also Ask box. Three to five real questions here, marked up with FAQ schema, is how you appear in it.
